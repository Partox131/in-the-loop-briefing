The war with Iran

Since 28 February 2026

On 28 February 2026 the United States and Israel launched air strikes on Iran while talks over Iran's nuclear programme were still going on. The first strikes killed Iran's Supreme Leader, Ali Khamenei, and other senior officials. They followed Iran's violent crackdown on protests in January 2026 and the largest US military build-up in the region since 2003.

Iran hit back within hours with missiles and drones on Israel, US bases and Gulf states that host them, and attacked shipping in the Strait of Hormuz, the narrow sea lane that carries much of the world's oil. Iran-backed groups in Lebanon, Iraq and Yemen joined in.

What each side wants

  • United States: President Trump described the aim as regime change in Iran. Washington also says it wants to stop Iran ever getting a nuclear weapon and to reopen Hormuz.
  • Israel: Remove what it calls existential threats: Iran's nuclear programme and its ballistic missiles.
  • Iran: Survive and make the war costly for the US and its allies. It now claims sovereignty over the Strait of Hormuz and says it will reopen it only if its conditions are met.

Where it stands

There is no ceasefire. Fighting is at a lower level than in the spring, but ships in and around Hormuz are still being hit (about 31 attacks in October, the most of the war, per UKMTO) and only a trickle of tankers gets through, which keeps oil prices high. Qatari mediators visited Tehran on 20 November.

In numbers

  • 3,636 killed in Iran in the first five weeks, 1,701 of them civilians (HRANA (rights group), via The Boston Report, 7 April 2026)
  • 23 US service members killed, 866 wounded (Pentagon, via The Boston Report, 4 November 2026)
  • 71 killed in Israel (31 civilians, 40 soldiers) (Israeli figures, via Wikipedia, 1 September 2026)
  • 2.3 a day ships through Hormuz (68 a day before the war) (IMF PortWatch, via The Boston Report, 15 November 2026)
  • $92.94 Brent oil per barrel ($71.32 the day before the war; above $138 in early April) (US EIA, via The Boston Report, 17 November 2026)

Timeline

  • 28 February 2026US and Israeli strikes begin; Ali Khamenei is killed. Iran fires back across the region.
  • 8 March 2026Mojtaba Khamenei, his son, is named Supreme Leader.
  • 8 April 2026A two-week ceasefire starts. Talks in Islamabad fail days later and the US blockades Iranian ports.
  • 17 June 2026The Islamabad Memorandum lifts both sides' blockades.
  • 7 July 2026After attacks on ships in Hormuz, Trump says the truce is over. The US blockade returns on 14 July.
  • 17 November 2026US jets strike an Iranian missile site on Qeshm Island; Iranian drones keep hitting Bahrain and Kuwait.

The Gaza war

Since 7 October 2023

On 7 October 2023 Hamas, the armed group that ran Gaza, led an attack on southern Israel that killed about 1,200 people and took around 250 hostages. Israel responded with a bombing campaign and a ground invasion of Gaza that destroyed much of the territory.

A ceasefire brokered by the US began on 10 October 2025. The living hostages were freed in its first days, but Israeli troops still hold part of Gaza behind a boundary called the Yellow Line, and the next steps (disarming Hamas, who governs Gaza, an international force) are stuck.

What each side wants

  • Israel: Make sure Hamas can never attack again. Defence Minister Israel Katz says Israel will not withdraw until Hamas is dismantled.
  • Hamas: Survive as a force, end the war and get Israel to withdraw fully. It has resisted giving up its weapons.
  • Palestinians in Gaza: Most of the 2 million people need shelter, food and medical care; many live in tents through winter storms.

Where it stands

The ceasefire is fragile. People are still killed most weeks, aid is limited (about 1,000 trucks a week in mid-November), and Israel's 18 November raid beyond the Yellow Line raised fears the deal could collapse.

In numbers

  • 74,695 Palestinians killed in Gaza since 7 Oct 2023 (Gaza Health Ministry, via Next Century Foundation, 14 November 2026)
  • 1,643 of them killed since the October 2025 ceasefire (Gaza Health Ministry, via Next Century Foundation, 14 November 2026)
  • 1,806 Israelis killed since 7 Oct 2023 (Israeli figures, via Next Century Foundation, 12 November 2026)

Timeline

  • 7 October 2023Hamas-led attack on Israel; Israel declares war.
  • 27 October 2023Israel's ground invasion of Gaza begins.
  • 10 October 2025US-brokered ceasefire takes effect.
  • 18 November 2026Israel's first ground operation beyond the Yellow Line since the ceasefire; Hamas calls it a flagrant violation.

Israel and Hezbollah in Lebanon

Since 2 March 2026

Two days into the Iran war, Hezbollah, the Iran-backed armed group and political party in Lebanon, fired on northern Israel for the first time since a 2024 ceasefire. Israel struck Beirut and the south, and sent ground troops into southern Lebanon from mid-March.

What each side wants

  • Israel: Push Hezbollah away from the border and hold a security zone up to the Litani River until it is no longer a threat.
  • Hezbollah: Force Israel to stop its attacks and leave Lebanese land. It rejected a US framework calling for its full disarmament.
  • Lebanon's government: Get Israeli troops out and bring the south under the Lebanese army.

Where it stands

A temporary ceasefire holds on paper, but Israeli air strikes continue and Israeli troops still hold parts of the south. The UN peacekeeping force's mandate ends on 31 December 2026.

In numbers

  • 4,390 killed in Lebanon (civilians and fighters not separated) (Lebanese Health Ministry, via The Boston Report, 5 November 2026)
  • 1 million+ people displaced in Lebanon (Wikipedia, 1 October 2026)

Timeline

  • 2 March 2026Hezbollah fires on Israel; Israel strikes Beirut.
  • 16 March 2026Israeli ground operations in southern Lebanon begin.
  • 16 April 2026A 10-day Israel–Lebanon ceasefire starts and is later extended.
  • 20 July 2026Israel hands parts of its buffer zone to the Lebanese army.
